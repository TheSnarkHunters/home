Val's Adventures at Scure Mansion - Full Walkthrough

Chapter 1: Start

  1. Pick up tape in Road. Go to Manor.
  2. Pick up bucket and chocolate box in Forecourt. Try to open door.
  3. Try to open door in House Garden.
  4. Go to Maze. Pick up glove. Go back to House Garden.
  5. Go to Boathouse. Use glove on baits. Pick up ladder. Use chocolate box on water, then grab radio within 10 seconds. Pick up top hat. Go to Forecourt.
  6. Use ladder on window.

Chapter 2: Goblin

  1. Pick up arrow.
  2. Use glove on Goblin.
  3. Talk to Goblin and pick "I challenge you to...". Pick random choices until "rap battle" is available.
  4. Pick lines that rhyme with what you just said, and pick "Val's my name" when available.
  5. Talk to Goblin. Pick "I know your name", then "Durneec."
  6. Climb the ladder and enter the tower.

Chapter 3: The two towers

  1. Pick up bow.
  2. Look at the basket.
  3. Use radio on the basket, open the radio and use tape with radio.
  4. Pick up crank handle, then go back to Forecourt.
  5. Use crank handle with well crossbeam, then pick up crowbar and go to Road.
  6. Use crowbar on car. Pick up pogo stick. Go back to Tower Gloria.
  7. Use pogo stick on the hatch above Rattle.
  8. Combine arrow with rope, then arrow and rope with bow.
  9. Use bow and arrow and rope with window.

Chapter 4: Through the halls

  1. Pick up remote.
  2. Go downstairs. Notice the flower pot on the right.
  3. Go into the Hallway.
  4. Open Bathroom door and go in.
  5. Pick up first aid kit and floss.
  6. Go back to the Hallway, then go downstairs unto the Hall.
  7. Go into the Waiting Room door.
  8. Talk to Virgil, pick "can I get into the graveyard", then "not even a hit-and-run?", then "I died."
  9. Use stereo, then go back to the Hall.
  10. Enter the Corridor.
  11. Enter the Gym, and pick up the Fertilizer from towel bag, then return to the Corridor.
  12. Enter the Library.
  13. Pick up paper and pencil, then go back upstairs.
  14. Use paper with Rob's bedroom door, then use pencil on door.
  15. Enter Rob's bedroom. Pick up plane and poster.
  16. Exit the bedroom, and go to your left. Enter the Tower and use the Fertilizer on the flower pot (see step 2 / step 11 — check this cross-reference).
  17. Pick up the Chamomile flower, then return outside.
  18. Go back to the Boathouse. Use remote on boat, then pick up batteries. Use pin on balloon, then pick up flat balloon.
  19. Use flat balloon with plasters.
  20. Go back to the Hall. Use balloon with carpet.
  21. Go to Corridor. Use battery with mouse trap.
  22. Go into the Gym. Try to pick up the mouse.
  23. Go back to the Corridor. Pick up trapped mouse.
  24. Go to the Kitchen. Use mouse on mum.
  25. Go back to the Kitchen. Look at magnet on fridge. Close the door. Pick up funnel, chilli pepper jar, vinegar, magnet and cutlery.
  26. Use chamomile and charged balloon on train.
  27. Press train button.
  28. Go into Dining room. Pick up mug with tea and phonebook.
  29. Go to the Gym. Talk to dad.
  30. Pick choices to make him calm. When he is no longer lifting weights, use funnel on dad, then use mug with tea on funnel. Then use heart rate monitor on dad.
  31. Go to Waiting Room. Give heart rate monitor to Virgil, then go to Graveyard.
  32. Pick up stone, then go to the Hallway upstairs.
  33. Use knife on Washing Room door and enter.
  34. Pick up pig.
  35. Use chilli peppers on boiler.
  36. Go into Hallway 2. Pick up wrench.
  37. Go into Tower Gloria. Drop pig from window.
  38. Go to Forecourt. Pick up coins.
  39. Go to Phone room. Use floss and coins on phone, than use phone.
  40. Call Rob's number.
  41. Go to Rob's bedroom. Pick up phone from wardrobe.
  42. Pick up wool jumper.
  43. Go to Library. Close door.
  44. Examine Rob's phone in your inventory.
  45. Push "The Odissey" on the shelf on the left.

Chapter 5: The basement

  1. Go into the Lumberloom. Pick up empty bottle and paint bucket.
  2. Go into the Disco Entrance. Ask Dany if you can go into the Disco. Ask him about his horns.
  3. Go to the Phone Room. Use History book on hieroglyphs. Combine plane with battery. Use plane on candlestick.
  4. Go to Gym. Talk to dad and pick choices to make him angry. When he's angry enough, give him paint bucket.
  5. Use paint bucket (open) with top hat.
  6. Go to the Bathroom. Use empty bottle with toilet.
  7. Go into the Disco Entrance. Right click on top hat in your inventory to wear it.
  8. Go into the Disco. Use stone with disco ball to break it.
  9. Talk to Mary.
  10. Use bottle of water with vodka.
  11. Talk to Mary. Ask her about her music, how long she's been playing guitar, what she needs to tune her guitar.
  12. Give fork to Mary.
  13. Go to Rob's bedroom. Close the door. Enter the Bathroom through the hole in the wall.
  14. Pick up radio.
  15. Go to Waiting Room. Use plasters on speakers and tape on stereo. Use stereo.
  16. Go to Lumberloom. Enter the hole in the wall.
  17. Pick up painting.
  18. Enter grandad's Office.
  19. Pick up coke and plastic box.
  20. Look at papers on table.

Chapter 6: The dungeon

  1. Combine fork with floss to make slingshot. Combine slingshot with battery.
  2. Use slingshot with hole.
  3. Wait for mouse to be close to you and pick it up.
  4. Use mouse on chain.
  5. Pick up matchbox.
  6. Look at the door.
  7. Use magnet with key.

Chapter 7: Reckoning

  1. Go to Washing Room.
  2. Use pebble, jumper and painting with washing machine. Use washing machine.
  3. Pick up blue pebble from washing machine. Combine pebble with wrench and knife to make Della Scure's sword.
  4. Go upstairs in Tower Silvestro. Use plastic box with sleeping bag.
  5. Go to Graveyard. Use corkscrew with vinegar.
  6. Examine all gravestones.
  7. Go into the Hall. Use matchbox with fireplace, then use old paper with fireplace.
  8. Go to Road. Place Della Scure's sword next to his gravestone.
  9. Use Resurrection spell on gravestone.
  10. Pick "Count", "Federico Orlando Incantalupo Della Scure", "1756", "Gunpowder".
  11. Talk to Count Della Scure.
  12. Give coke to Count.
  13. Ask him if he is going to help you travel through the maze and what he wants.
  14. Give Rob's phone to Count Della Scure.
  15. Go to Maze.
  16. Follow map instructions to reach Garage. (No mushrooms, gnome, no mushrooms, fish, Jabberwocky flagpole, roses, broken sign, no birdhouse, mushrooms)
  17. Pick up charcoal outside the Garage.
  18. Enter the Garage.
  19. Use lightbulb to switch lights on.
  20. Combine Charcoal with Fertilizer, to obtain Gunpowder smell.
  21. Return to Count Federico Della Scure and give him the Gunpowder smell.
  22. Enter the Dining Room and talk to Mum.
  23. Right-click on Rob's phone in your inventory, and pick up "Voice recorder".
  24. Talk to Mum and pick the following questions: "Did you enjoy your chamomile tea?", "what does Virgil like to drink?", "I heard a squeaking noise earlier".
  25. Return to the Garage. Give Rob's phone to Beatrice.
  26. Pick up keycard from jacket and pills from poison bottle.
  27. Go to Basement. Use keycard on card reader.
  28. Go to Kitchen. Use keycard with microwave.
  29. Use remagnetized keycard with card reader.
  30. Enter the Lab. Use monitor and set "7:10".
  31. Go to Maze and talk to Durneec.
  32. Go back to Lab. Use monitor and set "9:15".
  33. Go to Washing Room.
  34. Use window to go into ledge.
  35. Use clock hands to reach Bedroom.
  36. Go to bed.
